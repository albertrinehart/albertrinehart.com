Mise

A private, zero-based budgeting app for iPhone.

RoleDesign and development
PlatformiPhone
Built withXcode
StatusIn progress

Why

Albert built Mise to budget on his own phone the zero-based way: every dollar gets a job before it’s spent.

It has no bank connections. Nothing leaves the phone, and that’s the point.

How it works

UnallocatedMoney without a job yet sits at the top, so nothing goes unplanned.
YNAB importBring an existing budget over instead of starting from zero.
Investment accountsReconcile the balance without logging every trade.
ThemesChange the look. Color only where it earns its place.

Principles

Clean, simple and easy to get around. Nothing gimmicky.

Fix how it works before adding another chart.

It should feel like Apple made it.

What’s next

iCloud sync, once the basics are right.
